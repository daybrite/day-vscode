## Creating a project

The wizard asks your `day` CLI what to ask, so the platforms and toolkits offered are the ones it
actually supports. You can scaffold an **app**, a reusable **piece** of interface, or a headless
**part**.

What you get is a working app rather than an empty directory: typed routes, sample pages, locales,
and the native host projects each mobile target builds through.

- [Creating a project](https://vscode.daybrite.dev/docs/new-project) — every question explained
- [Project structure](https://daybrite.dev/docs/project-structure/) — a tour of the scaffold
- [Pieces](https://daybrite.dev/docs/pieces/) · [Parts](https://daybrite.dev/docs/parts/)
